Can I edit audio without an account?

Yes. Audio Editor is designed to provide its core editing workflow without requiring you to create an account.

Which formats can I export?

Audio Editor supports MP3, M4A, WAV and FLAC conversion where supported by the selected workflow. You can also compress using different bitrates and sample rates.

Can I mix multiple tracks?

Yes. Multi-track audio mixing lets you combine layers and adjust their volume. This is a member feature.

Does the App need microphone access?

Only if you use the built-in recorder. You can decline or revoke microphone access in iOS Settings.

Where can I import audio from?

You can bring in local files, iCloud files, Apple Music items available to the App, video sources and existing Audio Editor projects.
