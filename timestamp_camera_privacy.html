How CODING APPLICATION handles information when you use Timestamp Camera Basic+ for attendance, duty and on-site check-ins.

Timestamp Camera Basic+ is an iOS camera utility provided by CODING APPLICATION. It helps you create check-in records with date, time, location and custom text. This policy explains what information the App can access, where it is processed, how it is shared and the choices available to you.

01 — SCOPE

Information covered by this policy

This policy applies to the Timestamp Camera Basic+ iOS app and this website. It covers information accessed through Apple system permissions, information related to App Store subscriptions, and information you voluntarily send to support.

The App does not require an account to create a timestamp record. You can use its basic camera features without giving us your name, email address or payment details.

02 — CAMERA & LOCATION

Permissions with a clear purpose

With your permission, the App may access your camera to preview and capture photos or videos. It may access your photo library when you choose an existing item, save a finished record or export a file. You can deny or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings; related features may then be unavailable.

If you choose a location or address overlay, the App may access your device location and use date, time zone, coordinates or a readable address to build the record you requested. Location is optional and is not collected simply because the App is installed. You can create a record without location details when you turn that feature off or decline location access.

Custom text, check-in notes, timestamp styles and other settings are used to render the record you configure. The App does not intentionally access contacts, messages, email, calendars or other unrelated personal content.

03 — ON-DEVICE PROCESSING

Your records stay under your control

Timestamp Camera Basic+ is designed to process camera frames, timestamp details, selected location information and settings on your device. The photos, videos and location details you create are not uploaded to a CODING APPLICATION server as part of the normal capture workflow.

Finished records are saved to the location you choose, such as your iPhone photo library, Files or another destination made available through Apple's system share tools. When you share a record, the receiving app or service handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Photos and videos may contain embedded metadata, including date, time or location, depending on your device settings and the options you select. Review a record before saving or sharing it, especially when it contains information about people, private places or work sites.

04 — APP STORE & SUPPORT

Purchases and information you provide

If you subscribe to premium features, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We do not receive your full bank card number, payment password or other payment credentials. To verify and manage access, the App may handle subscription status, product identifier, transaction or order status, device information and related technical data supplied by Apple or stored on your device.

Subscription billing, automatic renewal, cancellation and refunds are governed by the checkout information and Apple's App Store policies. See the Apple Subscription Service Agreement for details.

If you contact support, we receive the email address you use and the information you include. We use it to respond, investigate the issue and maintain support records as reasonably necessary.

05 — SHARING & PROHIBITED USES

We do not sell personal information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use photos, videos, location records or other personal content for advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, data brokerage or training AI models.

We may disclose limited information when required by law, to protect users and the service from security threats, or to providers acting under our instructions. Content in a record is shared with third parties only when you choose to save it to or send it through those third-party services.

You are responsible for obtaining any consent required before photographing people, recording a location or sharing a record. Use the App only in accordance with applicable law and the rules of the places and services where you use it.

06 — SECURITY & RETENTION

Protecting information

We minimize the information the App requests, rely on Apple's permission and device security controls, and use encrypted connections for website and support communications where applicable. No system is completely secure, but the product is designed to avoid collecting camera content and location records on our servers.

On-device records remain until you delete them or remove them through the relevant iOS app or storage location. Locally stored subscription information remains as needed to verify access. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain service records and meet legal obligations.

07 — YOUR CHOICES

Permissions and control

  • Manage camera, photo library and location permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Turn location overlays off when a record should contain only date and time.
  • Review, edit, save, share or delete each record using the controls on your device.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings under Subscriptions.
  • Contact us to ask about support information you previously provided.

08 — INTERNATIONAL & CHILDREN

General provisions

Timestamp Camera Basic+ is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. If local law gives you additional privacy rights, you may contact us to exercise them. Apple and any app or service you use to save or share a record may process information in other countries under their own policies.

09 — UPDATES

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, legal requirements or data practices change. We will update the date at the top and provide additional notice when appropriate. Your continued use of the App after an update becomes effective means that you accept the updated policy to the extent permitted by law.
