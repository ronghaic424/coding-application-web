How CODING APPLICATION handles information when you use Audio Editor to edit, record and export audio on iPhone.

App: Audio EditorPackage name: com.code.audioeditorLast updated: November 22, 2026Effective date: November 22, 2026

Audio Editor is an iOS audio utility provided by CODING APPLICATION. It helps you trim, join, mix, record, convert, compress and export audio files. This policy explains what information the App can access, how it is used, where it is processed and the choices available to you.

01 — PERMISSIONS

Access with a clear purpose

Audio Editor may ask for access to Files, iCloud Drive, your local media library or other iOS file-picking locations when you choose an audio or video source to open or save. If you choose an Apple Music item that the system makes available to the App, the App uses that selected item for the import you requested. The App may request microphone access only when you use the built-in recorder. You can deny or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings, although related features may then be unavailable.

The App does not need access to your contacts, messages, email, calendars or unrelated personal content. It only works with the files, videos, recordings and settings you choose to use in the editing workflow. Items with platform, DRM or provider restrictions may not be available for import.

02 — AUDIO PROCESSING

Your edits stay focused

Audio Editor is designed to process imported audio, extracted video soundtracks, recordings, waveform information, multi-track layers and editing settings on your device as part of the normal editing workflow. The audio and video files you select are not uploaded to a CODING APPLICATION server for editing.

The App may create temporary working data so that waveform display, playback, undo, preview, mixing, format conversion and export can function. This data is used for the active editing experience and is removed or replaced according to the App's local storage behavior and the actions you take.

Audio files can contain metadata such as title, artist, album, date or location depending on the source file. Review the file and its metadata before sharing it with another person or service.

03 — STORAGE & EXPORT

You choose where the result goes

Finished exports are saved to the destination you select through Apple's Files, iCloud Drive, photo library or share tools, depending on the format and feature you use. The App may convert files to or from MP3, M4A, WAV and FLAC where supported, and may calculate a compressed file size from the bitrate and sample rate you choose. Once an exported file is sent to another app or service, that recipient handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Local projects, imported files, recent items, recordings and editing preferences remain on your device until you remove them through the App, iOS or the storage location that contains them. Audio Editor does not require an account for its basic editing workflow, including importing an existing Audio Editor project for further editing.

04 — APP STORE & SUPPORT

Purchases and information you provide

If you subscribe to premium features, including the multi-track audio mixing feature, Apple processes payment through the App Store. We do not receive your full bank card number, payment password or other payment credentials. To verify and manage access, the App may handle subscription status, product identifier, transaction or order status, device information and related technical data supplied by Apple or stored on your device.

Subscription billing, automatic renewal, cancellation and refunds are governed by the checkout information and Apple's App Store policies. See the Apple Subscription Service Agreement for details.

If you contact support, we receive the email address you use and the information you include. We use it to respond, investigate the issue and maintain support records as reasonably necessary.

05 — SHARING

We do not sell personal information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use your audio files, recordings or editing projects for advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, data brokerage or training AI models.

We may disclose limited information when required by law, to protect users and the service from security threats, or to providers acting under our instructions. Content in an audio file is shared with third parties only when you choose to save it to or send it through those third-party services.

You are responsible for obtaining any consent required before recording people, editing copyrighted material or sharing an audio file. Use the App only in accordance with applicable law and the rules of the services where you publish or send a file.

06 — SECURITY & RETENTION

Protecting information

We minimize the information the App requests, rely on Apple's permission and device security controls, and use encrypted connections for website and support communications where applicable. No system is completely secure, but the product is designed to avoid collecting audio content on our servers.

On-device audio files and projects remain until you delete them or remove them through the relevant iOS app or storage location. Locally stored subscription information remains as needed to verify access. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain service records and meet legal obligations.

07 — YOUR CHOICES

Permissions and control

  • Manage microphone, Files, iCloud Drive, photo library and media library permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Choose which audio or video files to open, edit, convert, export or share. You can leave any item unchanged.
  • Delete local projects, exports or temporary files through the App or the storage location that contains them.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions from your Apple ID settings under Subscriptions.
  • Contact us to ask about support information you previously provided.

08 — INTERNATIONAL & CHILDREN

General provisions

Audio Editor is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. If local law gives you additional privacy rights, you may contact us to exercise them. Information may be processed in countries where CODING APPLICATION or its service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

09 — UPDATES

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the product, legal requirements or data practices change. We will update the date at the top and provide additional notice when appropriate. Your continued use of Audio Editor after an updated policy becomes effective means that you accept the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.

10 — CONTACT

CODING APPLICATION

Questions or requests can be sent to mask@apptlsai.com. Please include “Audio Editor Privacy” in the subject line and avoid sending an audio file unless it is needed to investigate your request.

At a glance: Audio Editor is designed to work with the audio or video you choose on your iPhone. Apple handles App Store payments, and you decide whether to grant microphone or media access and where to send an export.
