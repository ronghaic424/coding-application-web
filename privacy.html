11. CCPA Privacy

Your Rights under the CCPA

If You are a California resident, You have the following rights:

  • The right to notice — to be informed which categories of Personal Data are collected and for what purposes.
  • The right to access — to request information regarding disclosure of Your Personal Data collected in the past 12 months.
  • The right to say no to the sale of Personal Data — to ask the Company not to sell Your Personal Data to third parties.
  • The right to know — to request details on categories collected, sources, purposes, and third parties with whom We share data.
  • The right to delete — to request deletion of Personal Data collected in the past 12 months.
  • The right not to be discriminated against — exercising any of the above rights will not result in denial of goods or services or different pricing.

We do not sell personal information. However, some Service Providers we partner with (for example, advertising partners) may use technology on the Service that "sells" personal information as defined by CCPA. To opt out of interest-based advertising on mobile devices:

To exercise your CCPA rights, contact Us using the information in the Contact section. We will respond within 45 days of receiving a verifiable request (extendable by a further 45 days with notice).
