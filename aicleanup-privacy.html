Privacy Policy

How CODING APPLICATION handles information when you use AI Cleanup.

Effective August 5, 2026Last updated August 5, 2026
On this pageOverviewOn-device dataCleanup processingSharingSecurityYour choicesContact

AI Cleanup is an iOS utility provided by CODING APPLICATION. The app helps you review photos and videos, compress selected media and optimize available device memory. This policy explains the information the app accesses, how it is used and the choices available to you.

01 — SCOPE

Information covered by this policy

This policy applies to the AI Cleanup iOS app and this website. It covers information accessed through Apple system permissions and information you voluntarily send to support.

02 — ON-DEVICE FEATURES

Photos, videos and storage information

With your permission, AI Cleanup may access the photos and videos you choose to review for cleanup, compression or memory optimization features. The app may use information such as media similarity, quality, file size and date to present cleanup suggestions.

This analysis occurs on your device. AI Cleanup does not upload your selected photo or video library to a CODING APPLICATION server. Changes occur only after an action you take and remain subject to the recovery behavior provided by iOS and the relevant system app.

03 — CLEANUP PROCESSING

Review, compress and optimize with your approval

AI Cleanup keeps cleanup candidates and available memory suggestions in the active app experience so you can review them. The app does not remove, compress or optimize content without an action you select and confirm.

When you use compression cleanup, the app processes the selected media for that visible feature. When you use memory optimization, the app processes the device information required to show and carry out that visible optimization flow.

04 — SUPPORT & WEBSITE

Information you provide directly

If you email support, we receive the address you use and the information you include. We use it to respond, investigate the issue and maintain support records as reasonably necessary.

This informational website does not require an account or request access to your photo library. A hosting provider may process basic technical logs for delivery, reliability and security.

05 — SHARING & PROHIBITED USES

We do not sell personal information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use your photos, videos or other personal content for advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, credit decisions, data brokerage or training AI models.

We may disclose limited information only when required by law, to protect users and the service from security threats, or to providers acting under our instructions.

06 — SECURITY & RETENTION

Protecting information

We minimize the information accessed, use Apple's iOS permission protections and use encrypted connections for website and support services. No system is completely secure, but the service is designed to avoid collecting personal media on our servers.

On-device cleanup candidates are retained only as needed for the active app experience. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to respond and meet legal obligations.

07 — YOUR CHOICES

Permissions and control

  • Manage photo and video permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Choose which cleanup candidates to review, compress or optimize.
  • Leave any item unchanged when it does not match your intent.
  • Contact us to ask about support information you previously provided.

08 — INTERNATIONAL & CHILDREN

General provisions

AI Cleanup is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. If local law gives you additional privacy rights, you may contact us to exercise them.

09 — UPDATES

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the product, legal requirements or data practices change. We will update the date at the top and provide additional notice when appropriate.
