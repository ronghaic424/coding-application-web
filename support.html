CLEANUP HELP

Review, compress and optimize.

AI Cleanup keeps the important decisions visible. Review the suggested items and confirm the action that matches your goal.

01

Photo and video review

Give photo access when you want to review media for cleanup. You can manage this permission in iOS Settings.

02

Compression cleanup

Select the large photos or videos you want to process, review the result and confirm the compression action.

03

Memory optimization

Use the memory optimization tools to review device pressure and make more room for your everyday apps and media.

Why does AI Cleanup need photo access?

Photo access lets the app review the photos and videos you choose to include in cleanup, compression or memory optimization features. You can deny or change this permission in iOS Settings.

How does compression cleanup work?

AI Cleanup shows the media available for review. Select the photos or videos you want to process, review the action and confirm it when you are ready.

What does memory optimization do?

Memory optimization helps you review the device space and memory information used by the cleanup flow, then choose the available optimization action that matches your needs.

Does AI Cleanup remove content automatically?

No. AI Cleanup keeps the review step visible and waits for you to select and confirm a cleanup action.

How do I manage an App Store subscription?

Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Billing and cancellation are managed by Apple.
