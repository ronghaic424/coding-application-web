GMAIL HELP

Connection and access controls

Gmail access is optional. You can disconnect inside the app or revoke access from your Google Account whenever you choose.

01

Connect Gmail

Open Gmail Cleanup, choose “Sign in with Google,” review Google's consent screen and grant access.

02

Disconnect in the app

Use the More menu and choose “Sign out of Google Mail.” This removes the OAuth token stored by AI Cleanup.

03

Revoke from Google

Open Google Account connections, select AI Cleanup and remove access.

Why does Google ask me to grant Gmail access?

AI Cleanup needs Gmail API permission to find the cleanup categories shown in the app and move messages you confirm to Trash. It requests gmail.modify because narrower read-only permissions cannot perform that user-requested move.

Why do I see an “unverified app” warning?

Google displays this warning while an app requesting Gmail access has not completed the applicable OAuth verification. Public access should be enabled only after the required review is complete.

Can AI Cleanup permanently delete Gmail messages?

No. AI Cleanup moves only selected and confirmed messages to Gmail Trash. Manage or recover those messages directly in Gmail.

Why does the Gmail list load in pages?

Messages load in groups of 25 so the first results appear sooner and the app avoids unnecessary Gmail API requests.

How do I manage an App Store subscription?

Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Billing and cancellation are managed by Apple.
