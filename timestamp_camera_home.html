Can I use Timestamp Camera Basic+ without GPS?

Yes. Location is optional. You can keep the date and time record while leaving location details out of the check-in.

Can I use it for attendance and duty check-ins?

Yes. Timestamp Camera Basic+ is designed for attendance, duty shifts, work sites and other check-in records that need clear time and location details.

Can I add a note to a check-in?

Yes. The App may provide controls for format, color, size, position and supporting text so you can include a site, shift or other note.
