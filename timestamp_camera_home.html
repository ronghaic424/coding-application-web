PRIVACY BY DEFAULT

The camera should belong to the person holding it.

Timestamp Camera requests access only for features you choose to use. You decide whether location is included, what appears on a frame and where the finished file goes.

⌁

Permission with purpose

Camera, photo library and location access are tied to visible features. You can change those permissions in iOS settings at any time.

◎

Location is your choice

GPS can be useful for a job site or a trip, but it should never be a surprise. Turn it off when a frame should stay location-free.

↗

Share on your terms

Save or share through the system tools you already trust. The app is built around creating a file you can review before it leaves your device.

Can I use Timestamp Camera without GPS?

Yes. Location is optional. You can keep the date and time stamp while leaving location details off the image.

Can I use it for video?

Timestamp Camera is designed for both photos and videos. Available capture options may depend on the device and the version of the App.

Can I change the timestamp style?

Yes. The App may provide controls for format, color, size, position and supporting text so the overlay fits your use.
