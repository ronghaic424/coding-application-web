PRIVACY BY DEFAULT

Useful cleanup without giving up control.

AI Cleanup asks only for the permissions required by the cleanup tools you choose. Your media stays under your control throughout the review and cleanup flow.

01

On-device by design

Photo and video review is designed around the content you choose on your iPhone. The app does not need to upload your library to a CODING APPLICATION server.

02

You approve every change

AI Cleanup presents review candidates and cleanup options. Nothing is removed, compressed or optimized until you select and confirm an action.

03

Permissions with purpose

Photo access and other permissions are tied to visible cleanup features. You can manage them in iOS Settings.

Can AI Cleanup clean photos and videos?

Yes. AI Cleanup helps you review photos and videos, then choose which items to clean up.

Can it compress large media?

Yes. Compression cleanup helps reduce the size of selected photos and videos when you confirm the action.

Does AI Cleanup optimize iPhone memory?

Yes. The app includes memory optimization tools to help you review storage pressure and make more room for everyday use.

Does the app remove content automatically?

No. AI Cleanup keeps the review step visible and waits for you to select and confirm a cleanup action.
